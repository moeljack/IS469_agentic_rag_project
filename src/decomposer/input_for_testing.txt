================================================================================

IRAS SINGAPORE CORPORATE INCOME TAX - TEST PROMPTS FOR INTAKE_PLANNER.PY

================================================================================



1. COMPUTATION (Tax Payable & Relief Calculations)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Tests extracting `chargeable_income`, `ya`/`fye`, and expanding sub-queries to

include Partial Tax Exemption (PTE), Start-Up Tax Exemption (SUTE), and CIT Rebates.



[Standard PTE + CIT Rebate]

"Our company has a chargeable income of S$450,000 for YA 2026. How much corporate income tax do we need to pay after the Partial Tax Exemption and CIT Rebate?"



[New Start-Up Computation (SUTE)]

"We are a newly incorporated company in 2024 with S$180,000 chargeable income in YA 2026. Assuming we qualify for the Start-Up Tax Exemption, what is our net tax payable?"



[Implicit YA via FYE (Tests `_normalise` deriving YA = FYE year + 1)]

"Calculate the corporate tax payable for a company whose financial year ended on 31 December 2025 with a chargeable income of S$85,000."



[Missing Income (Triggers `Clarify` for `chargeable_income`)]

"Can you help me calculate how much corporate tax my Pte Ltd has to pay for YA 2026?"





2. ELIGIBILITY (Qualifying Conditions for Schemes & Forms)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Tests extracting company attributes (`is_new_company`, `incorporation_year`,

revenue caps) and routing to statutory conditions.



[Start-Up Tax Exemption (SUTE) Shareholding Rule]

"We incorporated our Pte Ltd in 2025 with two corporate shareholders holding 50% each. Do we qualify for the Start-Up Tax Exemption for YA 2026?"



[Investment Holding Company Exclusion]

"Our newly incorporated company only holds commercial properties for rental income. Are we eligible to claim the Start-Up Tax Exemption?"



[Form C-S vs. Form C-S (Lite) vs. Form C]

"Our Singapore-incorporated company had an annual revenue of S$180,000 in FY 2025 and only claims capital allowances. Can we file Form C-S (Lite) for YA 2026?"



[Foreign Tax Credit / Unabsorbed Losses]

"Our company had a 60% change in shareholders this year. Are we still eligible to carry forward our unabsorbed trade losses from YA 2024?"



[Missing Incorporation Status (Triggers `Clarify` for `incorporation`)]

"Does my company qualify for the Start-Up Tax Exemption (SUTE) for YA 2026?"





3. DEADLINE (Filing & Payment Due Dates)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Tests extracting `fye` (Financial Year End) and distinguishing between Estimated

Chargeable Income (ECI) and Corporate Income Tax Return (Form C-S / Form C).



[ECI Filing Deadline (3 months from FYE)]

"If our company's financial year end is 31 March 2026, when is the deadline to file our Estimated Chargeable Income (ECI)?"



[Form C-S / Form C Filing Deadline]

"Our financial year ended on 30 September 2025. What is the due date for filing our YA 2026 Form C-S?"



[ECI Waiver Deadline Check]

"Our FYE is 31 December 2025 and our revenue is S$3 million with zero chargeable income. By what date do we need to file ECI, or are we waived?"



[Missing FYE (Triggers `Clarify` for `fye`)]

"When is the deadline to submit our ECI to IRAS?"





4. LOOKUP (Tax Rates, Deductibility Rules & Caps)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Tests general knowledge retrieval where no personal calculation or

missing-entity clarification is needed.



[Headline Rate & Exemption Tiers]

"What is the current headline corporate income tax rate in Singapore, and how do the Partial Tax Exemption tiers work for YA 2026?"



[Business Expense Deductibility (Medical Cap)]

"What is the cap on tax deductions for staff medical expenses if our company does not implement Portable Medical Benefits?"



[Section 14Q (Renovation & Refurbishment)]

"What is the expenditure cap over a 3-year period for Section 14Q renovation and refurbishment tax deductions, and are designer fees included?"



[Capital Allowances (PIC / Automation)]

"Can a company claim 100% write-off in one year for computers and prescribed automation equipment under capital allowances?"



[Enterprise Innovation Scheme (EIS)]

"What is the enhanced tax deduction rate for qualifying R&D expenditure conducted in Singapore under the Enterprise Innovation Scheme?"





5. PROCEDURE (How to File, Amend, or Claim)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Tests step-by-step administrative workflows on myTax Portal.



[Waiver to File Form C-S (Dormant Company)]

"Our company was dormant throughout FY 2025 with zero revenue. How do we apply for a waiver to file Form C-S on myTax Portal?"



[Revising an ECI / Objecting to Notice of Assessment (NOA)]

"We realized our actual chargeable income is much lower than the ECI we submitted last month. How do we file a revised ECI or object to a Notice of Assessment?"



[Corppass Authorisation for Tax Filing]

"How do I authorize our external tax agent via Corppass to submit Form C-S on behalf of our company?"





6. OUT_OF_SCOPE (Boundary Tests for `Refuse`)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Tests that the model does not get tricked by other Singapore taxes (handled by

IRAS, but not Corporate Income Tax) or foreign corporate taxes.



[GST Trap (IRAS, but not Corporate Income Tax)]

"Our company's taxable turnover just crossed S$1 million over the past 12 months. How do we register for GST with IRAS and is the rate still 9%?"



[Personal Income Tax Director Trap]

"As the sole director of a Pte Ltd earning S$120,000 in director's fees and salary, how much personal income tax do I need to pay for YA 2026?"



[Property Tax / Stamp Duty Trap]

"How much Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty (ABSD) and commercial property tax does a company pay when buying an office unit in Singapore?"



[Foreign Jurisdiction Trap]

"How much corporate tax does our subsidiary incorporated in Malaysia pay to LHDN for FY 2025?"

   Turn 2:   "S$150,000"

   Output 2: Plan(chargeable_income=150000)

   Turn 2:   "2025-12-31"
